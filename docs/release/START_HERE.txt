VERDANCE'S ASHES - START HERE
=============================

The green is dying. Ashwalkers hold the line.
This is a campaign shell for Beyond All Reason (public alpha).

ONE PATH
--------
1. Install Beyond All Reason from beyondallreason.info, start it once and
   let its launcher finish downloading (the engine and the game). You can
   close BAR again after that. Keep BAR closed while you play this.
2. Unzip this folder somewhere you can write, e.g. Documents\VerdanceAshes
   (not Program Files).
3. Double-click SETUP.bat (once). If Windows says "Windows protected your
   PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway". SETUP finds BAR (and asks for
   the path to BAR's "data" folder if it cannot), installs the campaign into
   BAR, and writes mission 1.
4. Double-click PLAY.bat. Your browser opens the war map at
   http://127.0.0.1:8770. Leave the PLAY window open while you play.
     - Choose a green sortie on the map, mark the target and the pad
     - Ready the drop: BAR starts loading the field in the background
     - Confirm the party, confirm the reserve, hire mercenaries if you like,
       then Lock drop: the company lands as soon as the field is ready
     - Switch to BAR and unpause to start
   The first drop on a new map downloads that map from BAR's content
   service (about 100 MB); the hub shows the progress.
   RESET.bat starts the campaign over (old save kept in state\backups).
5. Play the sortie in BAR. Space dismisses the briefing.
   After a win, 1/2/3 can keep fighting on the same map.
5. When the match ends, the kernel bridge brings the result home.
   If the map does not move: After the fight - bring it home
6. Talk, rest, hire mercs, or take the next drop.

Python is embedded under runtime\: nothing else to install.

IN-GAME SHORTCUTS
-----------------
  /vastory   story panel
  /vazones   print the loaded zone map (the minimap paints the company's
             BELIEF about each zone - what it thinks it holds and fears -
             not the ground truth; line of sight confirms or surprises)
  /vahelp    list commands
