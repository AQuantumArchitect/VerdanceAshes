fields. Hermitian, positive semi-definite and unit-trace by construction.

corridor, so coherence here means what it says: two places are entangled
when the same power's reach runs through both.

## Where these shapes would land

Pointers for the translation pass, once shapes are chosen here.
Nothing below has been changed.

- Temperaments -> `tick_zone`'s per-actor growth rates and
  `offscreen_clash_v1.ACTOR_BIAS`, keyed off `state["hydrology"]`,
  which no engine system reads today.
- The Armada-NullMercs front -> `run_offscreen_clashes`, whose
  SOFT_CONTROLS currently exempts armada ground from ever flipping.
- The player's transition -> `apply_front_decay`, whose pressure
  gate (0.35) never opens in a fresh campaign.
- The bloom -> `reset_live_raptors_but_escalate` and the hazard
  model, fed by lagged waterline recession.
- Counterfactual weather -> the `HYDRO_RING` offsets (data change).
- The seasons -> an amplitude term on the circulation in
  `ensure_planet_hydrology` / `distribute_planet_water`, advanced
  per mission alongside the phase.
- The tides -> the campaign gadget: scripted in-mission flood
  pulses on the 10-20 minute scale while a map's line is rising.
- Koom Valley's cap -> the authored `koom_valley` zone-map
  waterline levels (flats above the capped line never flood).
- The Rock Jungle's karst -> the authored `the_rock_jungle`
  hydrology `submergedAt` curves, deepened to match the response.
- The wind -> the maps' real `minWind`/`maxWind` already drive eco
  in BAR; the season would set each mission's wind range in the
  generated startscript.
- Player impact -> the escalation ladder's scale windows and force
  generation, recalibrated against real playthrough data.
- The chosen opening draft -> an authored edit of
  `state/campaign_state.json` zoneMemory, owner-triggered.

Standing defects noted while reading the engine, untouched here:
`offscreenActors` is keyed entirely by retired zone ids (45 of 45
stale; `ensure_zone_memory`'s setdefault will add new-id entries
alongside the orphans rather than heal them), and `map_waterline`
truncates the float phase to an int in the intent payload.
