Who comes in hostile

continuous, no thresholds

Each wild hero's stance toward the party is the sum of their ties to it: bonds count up, tension, rivalry and friction count down. A seeded roll under the curve decides whether they turn up alive and on the enemy's side, like the Kiln (put them down, raise the wreck), or the way their entry says, a wreck for most. Bonded heroes also turn up less often: a tie to someone in the party pulls a bonded hero in at 0.45 of the strength it pulls a rival.

p(hostile) = 1 / (1 + e(stance + bias) / softness)

Bias slides from early to late between missions . The sliders preview here only; the game reads data/story/encounter_rules.json.
