A campaign for Beyond All Reason

The ashes remember commanders

You are an Ashwalker founding a new base in the Rock Jungle. Greenest Fields is the Tenebrium raid — crack Armada decoy farms so you can build at home. Two journeys, both real: dig in at the jungle first, or jump the Fields on the way. Plan here. Fight in Beyond All Reason.

  1. 1Form the Company on first open — core always ships; pick 3 unlocked packs. Unpicked heroes stay off your board until a field recruit.
  2. 2Pick a mission on the main screen, mark landing / objective / extraction, then Drop into battle.
  3. 3In BAR, take the objective. When the match ends, return here and bring it home.
  4. 4Camp (left bar) raises the jungle shelf. Home → Broker shows each available purchase and its exact cost.
  5. 5Save / Load / Reset in the footer manage campaign state.
