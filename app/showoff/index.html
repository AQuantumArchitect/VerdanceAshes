    You are an Ashwalker founding a new base in the Rock Jungle. Greenest Fields is the Tenebrium raid — crack Armada's decoy's farms so you can build at home. Two journeys, both real: dig in at the jungle first, or jump the Fields on the way. Plan here. Fight in Beyond All Reason.
