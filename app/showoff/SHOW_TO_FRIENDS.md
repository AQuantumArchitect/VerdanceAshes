# Verdance's Ashes — show this to friends

**Tagline:** The green is dying. Ashwalkers hold the line. Your aces. Your scars.

## 30-second pitch

A single-player campaign for **Beyond All Reason**. You plan the war from a
command map — aces, theaters, a Null Mercs broker. You fight short sorties in
BAR. While you look away, the planet digs in. It remembers what you did.

## Demo in 60 seconds

1. Run `app\bin\PLAY_CAMPAIGN.bat` (leave it open)
2. Browser opens the map — dismiss the welcome card
3. Pick a green **sortie** → confirm the objective → **Drop into battle**
4. Play the BAR mission (Space dismisses the briefing)
5. Back at the map: **After the fight — bring it home**

Map-only (no launch): open `index.html` or `SHOW_OFF.bat`.

## Why it's cool

- War-map campaign on top of a real RTS
- Aces with XP, wounds, and bonds
- Same-map continues keep your company together
- Living occupation and a Null Mercs vs Armada world war
